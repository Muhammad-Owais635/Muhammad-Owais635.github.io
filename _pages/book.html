About the book

One path from the basics to the frontier

Most resources either stop at classic neural networks or jump straight into large language models without the foundations. This book connects the two. Part I builds deep learning from the ground up: neurons, activation and loss functions, gradient descent, regularization, CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs and GRUs. Part II picks up at attention and the Transformer, then covers how large language models are trained, scaled, fine-tuned and aligned, how they reason, how they act as agents, and how to evaluate them and keep them safe.

It grew out of my teaching and research in natural language processing, and is written for readers who want to understand how these models actually work, not only how to call them.

Intuition first

Every concept opens with a plain-language picture, such as a hill descent for gradient descent or a library search for attention.

Maths made readable

Equations are introduced step by step and worked through with small numerical examples.

Runnable code

Keras and Python examples for perceptrons, CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs and GRUs that you can run and modify.

Up to date

Covers recent topics: Mixture of Experts, LoRA, RLHF and DPO, reasoning models, RAG, MCP and AI agents.

Who it's for

Written for learners and builders

Students

Undergraduate and graduate students taking a first course in deep learning or NLP.

Instructors

Teachers looking for a structured course text, chapter by chapter, from foundations to LLMs.

Practitioners

Engineers and researchers who use LLMs and want to understand what happens inside them.

Prerequisites: basic Python and high-school algebra. The book introduces everything else as it goes.
