UET ITEC 2025

University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Lahore, Pakistan February 12, 2025

I was invited to serve as a judge for the Project Exhibition at UET ITEC 2025, an in-house Talent Hunt and Technical Event organized in collaboration with industrial partners.

UET ITEC is a prominent national-level event that attracts participation from universities across Pakistan. In previous editions, students from more than 30 universities took part in this highly competitive and academically enriching event.

The event encompasses a wide range of activities involving students, faculty, alumni, and industry professionals, with a strong emphasis on emerging technologies, innovation, and practical skill development through competitions, seminars, and hands-on workshops.

As a judge, I had the opportunity to evaluate and interact with over 400 computing students from the main campus (Computer Science, Computer Engineering, and Electrical Engineering) as well as satellite campuses including Narowal, Kala Shah Kaku, Faisalabad, and Gujranwala.

Students showcased an impressive array of final year projects, semester projects, and postgraduate research contributions, reflecting both technical depth and creative problem-solving capabilities.
