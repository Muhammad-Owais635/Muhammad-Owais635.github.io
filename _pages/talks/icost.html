  • ICOSST 2024 Presentation

    18th International Conference on Open Source Systems and Technologies (ICOSST), IEEE Lahore, Pakistan (2024)

    • I presented my research paper titled FOID: A Feature-Optimized Intrusion Detection System for Securing IoMT Healthcare Networks at the prestigious 18th ICOSST Conference 2024.

      This work focuses on developing a highly efficient and optimized intrusion detection system tailored for Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) networks, addressing critical security vulnerabilities in healthcare IoT infrastructures.

      The presentation highlighted novel feature selection techniques and machine learning models designed to improve detection accuracy while preserving computational efficiency, ensuring robust defense against evolving cyber threats in medical environments.
