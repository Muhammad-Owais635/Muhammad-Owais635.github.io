Python Programming Guest Talk

TEVTA · Government College of Technology Railway Road, Lahore, Pakistan September 20, 2024

I was invited as a guest speaker at the Technical Education & Vocational Training Authority (TEVTA), Lahore, to deliver a talk to students enrolled in the Python programming course.

The presentation focused on the importance of Python and its diverse real-world applications across multiple industries, including web development, data science & AI, automation, cybersecurity, and software prototyping.

The goal of the session was to inspire students by highlighting career opportunities and encouraging them to engage in practical projects and deeper technical learning.
