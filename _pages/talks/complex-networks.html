Text-Based Social Bot Detection Leveraging Linguistic Structures and Sentiment Features

The 14th International Conference on Complex Networks and their Applications

Binghamton, New York, USA • Dec 11, 2025

I presented my research paper titled Text-Based Social Bot Detection Leveraging Linguistic Structures and Sentiment Features.

This work proposes a text-based approach for detecting social bots by analyzing how language is structured and how sentiment is expressed in user posts. The method combines linguistic patterns with sentiment dynamics to distinguish automated accounts from humans.

The results demonstrate that these features capture meaningful behavioral differences and significantly improve detection performance on real-world social media datasets.
