Complex Networks 2025

Binghamton University Binghamton, New York, USA December 11, 2025
View paper in Publications →

I presented my research paper titled Text-Based Social Bot Detection Leveraging Linguistic Structures and Sentiment Features at the prestigious Complex Networks Conference 2025.

In this work, I proposed a text-based approach for detecting social bots by analyzing how language is structured and how sentiment is expressed in user posts. The method combines linguistic patterns (such as syntax and word usage) with sentiment dynamics to distinguish automated accounts from humans.

The results show that these features capture meaningful behavioral differences and improve bot detection in real-world social media data.
