  • CodeEx 2024 Participation

    Namal University Mianwali, Pakistan (2024)

    • I had the pleasure of accompanying my talented students from Superior University Lahore to CodeEx 2024, hosted at Namal University, Mianwali.

      The multi-university competition brought together participants from eight leading institutions, engaging students in exciting challenges such as speed programming and creative poster design.

      It was a day filled with coding enthusiasm, creative energy, and academic pride. Representing our institution among brilliant minds from across Pakistan was truly an honor.

      We traveled from Lahore to Mianwali as a team, strengthening collaboration and passion for technology. The hospitality and academic environment at Namal University were exceptional.

      Our participation was recognized with certificates of participation and honorary shields, celebrating the students’ dedication and engagement.
