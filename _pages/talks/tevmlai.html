  • Machine Learning & AI Guest Talk

    ITEC Punjab Lahore, Pakistan (2024)

    • I was honored to be invited as a guest speaker at ITEC Punjab, Lahore, where I delivered a talk on Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence: Concepts and Applications.

      During the session, I introduced students to core ML and AI concepts, foundational algorithms, and their practical relevance across industries such as healthcare, finance, autonomous systems, and cybersecurity.

      The session aimed to demystify AI technologies and inspire students to explore this rapidly evolving field through hands-on learning and research.

      As a token of appreciation, I was awarded a certificate and an honorary shield for my contribution.
