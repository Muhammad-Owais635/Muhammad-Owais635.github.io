Expanding E-Khidmat Center’s Services to a Broader Citizen Base Using Intelligent Urdu Language Technology

CLE, KICS, UET Lahore Jan 2022 – Mar 2023 FCDO · PITB
Project details on the CLE website ↗
12government services voice-enabled
700citizens surveyed in 3 districts
2Punjabi dialects: Majhi & Shahpuri

A mobile, speech-enabled platform that lets citizens in Punjab ask, in Urdu, about government services offered at E-Khidmat Centers. For each service it tells them the fee, location, timings, required documents and procedure, spoken back in Urdu. Services were chosen from a needs survey of citizens, and the platform was evaluated with real users.

Objectives & Deliverables

Objectives

  1. Identify the information services that poor and marginalised communities in Punjab need most.
  2. Build a local-language, speech-enabled platform giving single-window access to those services.
  3. Assess users’ perception of the platform and the quality of the information it provides.

Deliverables

  • Literature review and needs-assessment survey tool
  • Design of the speech-enabled mobile e-government platform
  • Report on citizens’ information needs
  • Speech-enabled mobile platform for 12 services
  • User perception and experience report
  • Final project report
