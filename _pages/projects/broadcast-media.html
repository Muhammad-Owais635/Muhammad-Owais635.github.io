Automated Urdu Broadcast Media Content Extraction and Analytics

CLE, KICS, UET Lahore Jul 2021 – Aug 2023 TTSF · HEC · PEMRA
Project details on the CLE website ↗
SpeechUrdu ASR + speaker identification
Texton-screen Urdu OCR
Searchtime-stamped video search

A content-based audio-visual analysis system that helps users find relevant videos in large collections of online Urdu media. It transcribes both what is said and what is written on screen, with timestamps, and then generates analytics and trend reports from the text.

Objectives & Deliverables

Objectives

  1. Help news-media users extract and manage spoken and written Urdu content in online videos.
  2. Develop advanced content processing and intelligent analytics for Urdu.
  3. Give wider audiences access to Urdu video content through an online search engine.

Deliverables

  • System design document
  • Enhanced Urdu speech recognition system
  • Speaker identification system
  • Video transcription from the audio stream
  • Text-area detection, segmentation and Urdu OCR
  • Trending-topic identification system
  • Integrated content extraction and analytics system with test reports
