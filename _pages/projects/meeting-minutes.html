Automatic Meeting Minutes and Analytics Generation for Urdu

CLE, KICS, UET Lahore Oct 2022 – Oct 2024 TTSF · HEC
Project details on the CLE website ↗
15 hmulti-speaker meeting corpus
2languages: Urdu & English
4platforms: Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack

An intelligent meeting assistant that automatically transcribes meetings and turns them into insights: action items, topics, summaries and minutes. It runs as a browser extension for popular video-conferencing platforms, so businesses and government bodies can get meeting insights while keeping their data private.

My contribution

Designed and developed an intelligent automatic meeting-minutes system for Punjabi-accented Urdu and English speakers, including speech-separation models to handle overlapping audio. Built and curated a 15-hour multi-speaker meeting speech corpus to support speech processing and downstream applications.

Objectives & Deliverables

Objectives

  1. Build an intelligent note-taker for Urdu and English speakers with a Punjabi accent.
  2. Develop systems that detect and separate overlapping speech for speech-based applications.
  3. Create a 15-hour meeting speech corpus with multiple speakers.

Deliverables

  • System functional specification and design
  • Meeting data design, collection and annotation
  • Overlapped-speech detection and separation
  • Topic identification
  • Meeting summarization using acoustic and lexical features
  • Meeting minutes generation
  • Prototype system and user acceptance testing
