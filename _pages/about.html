About me

I am a doctoral researcher in Computer Science at Tokyo Metropolitan University, working in the Masaki Aida Laboratory. I specialise in online social network analysis through natural language processing, with expertise in large language models, machine learning, deep learning, speaker identification, and speech recognition. I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Engineering and Technology (UET), Lahore (2021–2023), where my thesis focused on detecting social bots to improve the reliability of social media. Before that, I earned my BS in Computer Science from the University of Sargodha (2017–2021).

My research focuses on building intelligent and interpretable AI systems for analysing human interaction and information dynamics in online environments: detecting social bots and AI-generated text, studying echo chambers and misinformation. Before my PhD I built Urdu speech and language technology at the Center for Language Engineering, UET Lahore, and taught undergraduate computer science.
