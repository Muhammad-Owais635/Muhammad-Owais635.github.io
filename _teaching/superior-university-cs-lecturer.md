As a **Computer Science Lecturer** at Gold Campus, Superior University Lahore (Aug 2023 – Feb 2025), I taught foundational courses to undergraduate students, focusing on cultivating programming skills and logical reasoning.

## Courses Taught

### 📘 Programming Fundamentals
- Introduced students to programming concepts using Python.
- Guided them through problem-solving techniques and control structures.
- Designed interactive programming exercises to enhance logic development.

### 🧱 Object-Oriented Programming
- Delivered comprehensive sessions on OOP concepts including encapsulation, inheritance, polymorphism, and abstraction.
- Applied real-world examples and Java/Python for practical understanding.

### 🔗 Data Structures and Algorithms
- Covered core data structures: arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, and graphs.
- Taught searching, sorting, and algorithmic design techniques.

### 🤝 Academic Collaboration
- Actively engaged in collaborative research on Intrusion Detection in IoMT networks using deep reinforcement learning.
- Mentored and advised students on mini-projects integrating AI/ML approaches.
