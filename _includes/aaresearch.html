Research Projects

  • Oct 2022 – Oct 2024

    Automatic Meeting Minutes and Analytics Generation for Urdu

    Center for Language Engineering (CLE), KICS, UET, Lahore

    Designed and developed an intelligent automatic meeting minutes generation system for Punjabi, Urdu and English speakers, including speech separation models to handle overlapping audio. Built and curated a 15-hour multi-speaker meeting speech corpus to support speech processing and downstream applications.

  • Jul 2021 – Aug 2023

    Automated Urdu Broadcast Media Content Extraction and Analytics

    Center for Language Engineering (CLE), KICS, UET, Lahore

    Developed a system to enable efficient extraction and management of spoken and written Urdu content from online digital videos. Implemented advanced content processing and intelligent analytics techniques for Urdu language data. Enhanced accessibility by enabling users to search and retrieve Urdu video content through an online search platform.

  • Jan 2022 – Mar 2023

    Expanding E-Khidmat Center’s Services and Delivery to Broader Citizen Base Using Intelligent Urdu Language Technology

    Center for Language Engineering (CLE), KICS, UET, Lahore

    Developed an intelligent, speech-enabled service delivery platform in local languages to improve accessibility and streamline access to public services. Evaluated user interactions and feedback to continuously enhance system usability, relevance, and overall service effectiveness.
