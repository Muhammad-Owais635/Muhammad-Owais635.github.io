Automatic Meeting Minutes and Analytics Generation for Urdu

Project details from the CLE research projects list.

15 hmulti-speaker meeting corpus
2languages: Urdu & English
4platforms: Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack

An intelligent meeting assistant that automatically transcribes meetings and turns them into insights: action items, topics, summaries and minutes. It runs as a browser extension for popular video-conferencing platforms, so businesses and government bodies can get meeting insights while keeping their data private.

My contribution

Designed and developed an intelligent automatic meeting-minutes system for Punjabi-accented Urdu and English speakers, including speech-separation models to handle overlapping audio. Built and curated a 15-hour multi-speaker meeting speech corpus to support speech processing and downstream applications.

Expanding E-Khidmat Center’s Services to a Broader Citizen Base Using Intelligent Urdu Language Technology

Project details from the CLE research projects list.

12government services voice-enabled
700citizens surveyed in 3 districts
2Punjabi dialects: Majhi & Shahpuri

A mobile, speech-enabled platform that lets citizens in Punjab ask, in Urdu, about government services offered at E-Khidmat Centers. For each service it tells them the fee, location, timings, required documents and procedure, spoken back in Urdu. Services were chosen from a needs survey of citizens, and the platform was evaluated with real users.

My contribution

Developed an intelligent, speech-enabled service delivery platform in local languages to improve accessibility and streamline access to public services. Evaluated user interactions and feedback to continuously enhance system usability, relevance and overall service effectiveness.

Automated Urdu Broadcast Media Content Extraction and Analytics

Project details from the CLE research projects list.

SpeechUrdu ASR + speaker identification
Texton-screen Urdu OCR
Searchtime-stamped video search

A content-based audio-visual analysis system that helps users find relevant videos in large collections of online Urdu media. It transcribes both what is said and what is written on screen, with timestamps, and then generates analytics and trend reports from the text.

My contribution

Developed a system to extract and manage spoken and written Urdu content from online digital videos. Implemented advanced content processing and intelligent analytics for Urdu language data, and made Urdu video content searchable and retrievable through an online search platform.
