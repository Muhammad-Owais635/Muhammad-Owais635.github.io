I was honored to be invited as a **guest speaker** at **ITEC Punjab, Lahore**, where I delivered a talk on **Machine Learning & Artificial Intelligence: Concepts and Applications**.

During the session, I introduced students to core **ML and AI concepts**, foundational algorithms, and their real-world impact in sectors like:

- **Healthcare**
- **Finance**
- **Autonomous Systems**
- **Cybersecurity**

The session aimed to **demystify AI** and ignite students’ curiosity to dive deeper into this rapidly evolving domain.

As a token of appreciation, I was awarded a **certificate** and an **honorary shield** for my contribution.
