I had the pleasure of accompanying my talented students from **Superior University Lahore** to **CodeEx 2024**, hosted at **Namal University, Mianwali**.

This multi-university competition brought together participants from **8 top institutions**, engaging in:

- 💻 **Speed Programming**
- 🎨 **Poster Design Competitions**

It was a day full of coding excitement, creative energy, and academic pride. Representing our university among brilliant minds from across Pakistan was a moment of honor.

---

### 🛣️ Journey & Achievement

We **traveled from Lahore to Mianwali** as a team, bonded by passion for technology and innovation. The hospitality and environment at Namal were remarkable.

We were honored with:

- 🏆 **Certificates of Participation**
- 🛡️ **Honorary Shields** for our contribution and engagement
