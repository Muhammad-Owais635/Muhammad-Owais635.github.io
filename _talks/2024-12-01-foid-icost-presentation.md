I presented my research paper titled **FOID: A Feature-Optimized Intrusion Detection System for Securing IoMT Healthcare Networks** at the prestigious **18th ICOSST Conference 2024**. 

This work focuses on developing a highly efficient and optimized intrusion detection system designed specifically for the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) networks, addressing critical security challenges faced by healthcare IoT devices.

The presentation covered the novel feature selection techniques and machine learning models employed to enhance detection accuracy while maintaining computational efficiency, offering robust protection against evolving cyber threats in medical environments.
