In 2024, I presented an engaging talk on **Machine Learning: Concepts and Applications** to students at ITEC Punjab, Lahore. 

The presentation introduced core machine learning principles, key algorithms, and real-world applications spanning industries such as healthcare, finance, and autonomous systems. 

The goal was to provide foundational knowledge and inspire learners to explore this transformative technology further.
