I was invited as a **guest speaker** at the **Technical Education & Vocational Training Authority (TEVTA)**, Lahore, to deliver a talk to students enrolled in the **Python programming course**.

My presentation focused on the **importance of Python** and its **diverse real-world applications** across industries such as:

- **Web Development**
- **Data Science & AI**
- **Automation & Scripting**
- **Cybersecurity**
- **Software Prototyping**

The goal was to **inspire students** about the career opportunities and the practical power of Python, encouraging them to pursue hands-on projects and further studies in technology fields.
