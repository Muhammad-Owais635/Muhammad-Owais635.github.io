I delivered a presentation to students enrolled in the Python course at ITEC Punjab, Lahore, focusing on the **importance of Python** and its diverse applications across multiple industries and job roles.

The talk covered Python's versatility in fields such as web development, data science, artificial intelligence, automation, and more, aiming to inspire students about the career opportunities and real-world uses of Python programming.
